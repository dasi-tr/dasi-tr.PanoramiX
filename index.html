FAQ

Questions and answers

What is HymmShot?

HymmShot is a growing family of focused screenshot tools for Windows. It starts with HymmScroll for long scrolling captures and HymmStack for combining separate areas into one image.

Which tool do I need?

Use HymmScroll for one continuous thing that extends beyond the screen. Use HymmStack when the information is spread across different windows, areas or steps.

Do HymmShot tools upload my screenshots?

No. Capturing and editing happen locally on your Windows PC.

Where do I download them?

HymmScroll and HymmStack are currently free and available from their official Microsoft Store listings.
