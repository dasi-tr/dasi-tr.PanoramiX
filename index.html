Frequently asked questions

The practical details.

What does HymmShot actually do?

HymmShot captures content that is longer than your screen as one continuous screenshot. After capturing, you can edit the image, hide private information, add annotations and save or export it.

Is HymmShot still in Beta?

Yes. HymmShot is currently in active Public Beta. Core capture, editing, privacy and export features are available now, while we continue improving long-capture stitching, especially on complex or dynamic content.

Is HymmShot free?

Yes. HymmShot is free to use during the Public Beta. Pricing may change after the Beta as the product becomes more mature, and any future pricing changes will be communicated clearly.

Does HymmShot work outside the browser?

Yes. It is designed for scrollable areas in Windows applications, including browsers, IDEs, Terminal, CMD, logs, chats and other windows.

Can I edit a screenshot I already have?

Yes. You do not have to capture it with HymmShot. Open an existing PNG, edit it, hide private information, add notes and export it.

Can I protect sensitive information?

Yes. Use pixelation or blur for selected passwords, names, IDs, email addresses, private text and confidential values before sharing.

Can I export the long screenshot to PDF?

Yes. HymmShot can export the result as a clean, continuous single-page PDF.

What happens if a long capture needs attention?

HymmShot can show detected issue locations. Use Previous / Next to review them, hide the issue indicators, or choose Recapture when needed.
