# About HymmShot

> HymmShot is a growing family of focused screenshot tools for Windows. Each tool is built around a clear capture workflow, allowing the family to expand without turning every app into an overloaded toolbox.

Page: https://hymmshot.com/about.html · Contact: contact@hymmshot.com · LinkedIn: https://www.linkedin.com/company/hymmshot

Built by Hadassa Goodman, a QA and automation lead who needed to capture long pages, chats and logs in environments where uploading a screenshot to a web service was not an option. Every tool runs locally on the user's own Windows PC, with a built-in editor that can blur sensitive details before anything is shared. The original problem came from QA work, but the tools are designed for a broad audience including legal professionals, content editors, researchers, customer support teams, technical users and everyday users.

Current tools: HymmScroll (https://hymmshot.com/hymmscroll/) and HymmStack (https://hymmshot.com/hymmstack/).
