What changed in HymmShot 2.0 Beta.

See the current HymmShot beta features, including scrolling capture, built-in editing and PDF export.

HymmShot 2.0 Beta

  • Scrolling capture remains the core workflow.
  • Captured content opens directly in a built-in editor.
  • Added precise cropping and real pixelation.
  • Added transparent highlighting and freehand drawing.
  • Added arrows, rectangles, circles and quick markers.
  • Added text annotations.
  • Added continuous single-page PDF export.

The public download remains a free Windows beta.
