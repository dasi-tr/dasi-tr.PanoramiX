# HymmStack — combine multiple screenshots into one image on Windows

> HymmStack captures several separate areas, from different windows, apps or steps, and combines them into a single image.

Page: https://hymmshot.com/hymmstack/ · Microsoft Store: https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9p1lzh1rvsdb · Contact: contact@hymmshot.com

## How it works
1. Select an area: any part of any window.
2. Add more: each capture can come from somewhere else.
3. Combine: HymmStack turns the captures into one image.
4. Finish: edit, blur, and copy or save the result.

## Where it works
Like a regular screenshot: anything visible on screen, in any app or website, can be captured.

## Editor
Blur, highlight, text, pen and eraser, shapes, crop, undo and redo. Copy the finished image to the clipboard, save it as an image, or export it as a PDF.

## When it is useful
Legal and professional work that needs details from separate screens in one image; content editing and research that combines excerpts or references; customer support and documentation; before-and-after comparisons; QA and development workflows; and everyday situations where several separate screenshots would be harder to follow.

## Privacy
Capturing and editing happen locally on the user's Windows PC. No upload, no account.
