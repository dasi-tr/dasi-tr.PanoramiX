Privacy

How the HymmShot website handles data.

Privacy information for the HymmShot website, including analytics and third-party links.

Website data and analytics

The website does not include account registration, contact forms or advertising scripts. It uses Google Analytics / Google tag for aggregate website traffic, interactions with Microsoft Store buttons and product-video engagement. Google Analytics may provide aggregated geographic reporting such as country, region and city based on its own processing; HymmShot does not send names, email addresses or exact personal location in custom event parameters. No additional analytics or advertising service has been added.

Third-party services

App-install links lead to Microsoft Store, while source-code links lead to GitHub. The footer also links to LinkedIn. Those services may process information under their own privacy policies.

Desktop application

This notice describes the website package published at hymmshot.com. Information about any future diagnostics, telemetry or network features in the Windows application should be documented with the relevant release.
