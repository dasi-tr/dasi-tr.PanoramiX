Privacy

How the HymmShot website handles data.

A concise explanation of website analytics, third-party links and how this notice relates to the desktop tools.

Website data and analytics

The website does not include account registration, contact forms or advertising scripts. It uses Google Analytics / Google tag for aggregate website traffic, interactions with Microsoft Store buttons and product-video engagement.

Google Analytics may provide aggregated geographic reporting such as country, region and city based on its own processing. HymmShot does not send names, email addresses or exact personal location in custom event parameters.

Third-party services

App-install links lead to Microsoft Store and the site also links to LinkedIn. Those services may process information under their own privacy policies.

Desktop applications

This notice describes the website published at hymmshot.com. Product-specific diagnostics, telemetry or network behavior should be documented with the relevant application release.
